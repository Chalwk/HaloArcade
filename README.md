# HaloArcade

A top-down shooter game built with Love2D, inspired by the Halo universe. Pilot a Spartan through waves of Covenant enemies while mastering different weapons and power-ups.

## Features

- **Multiple Weapons**: Assault Rifle, Plasma Pistol (with charge shot), and Rocket Launcher
- **Enemy Variety**: Fight Grunts, Elites, and Hunters with unique behaviors
- **Power-up System**: Collect health, shield, and weapon upgrades
- **Wave Progression**: Survive increasingly difficult waves of enemies
- **Shield Mechanics**: Strategic health/shield management system
- **Visual Effects**: Particle explosions and plasma trails

### Controls
- **WASD**: Move player
- **Space**: Fire weapon
- **1/2/3**: Switch weapons
- **Shift**: Charge Plasma Pistol
- **R**: Restart game

## Gameplay

- Defeat waves of Covenant enemies
- Manage your shields and health
- Collect power-ups for survival
- Progress through increasingly difficult waves
- Try to achieve the highest score!
